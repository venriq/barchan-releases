Barchan

Barchan is a viewer for HiRISE products, the very large images the High Resolution Imaging Science Experiment returns from Mars orbit.

Straight from the archive

Open a HiRISE product by its address, as the archive or the HiRISE website gives it. Barchan reads only the parts of the image on screen, so a product of hundreds of megabytes opens without downloading it first.

Browse the archive

The HiRISE Archive browser finds and opens images without leaving Barchan. Expand the archive's folders to see each image's date and exact size, filter them by name, and double-click an image to open it.

Reads PDS4 labels

Open a PDS4 label and Barchan opens the JPEG 2000 product it lists. The label's fields fill the Metadata section, where you can search them, and the readout gives calibrated values.

Measure on the surface

Click out a path and the Measurement section lists each leg's length and azimuth and the total. On a map projected product the lengths are in metres on the surface of Mars.

Anaglyphs in full resolution

HiRISE stereo anaglyphs open like any other product, at every scale down to single pixels. Put on red and cyan glasses and pan across the relief.

See what it is doing

The Activity window charts the last two minutes of decoding, network reads, cache hits, and memory, so a slow view can be traced to the network, the decoder, or the caches.
